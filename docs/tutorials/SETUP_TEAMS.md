# Tutorial: Setting Up Teams
## Step 1: Navigate to Teams
Click **Teams** in sidebar.
## Step 2: Create Team
Click **+ Create Team**.
- Name: Engineering
- Manager: Select manager
- Department: Engineering
## Step 3: Add Members
Click **Add Members** and select employees.
## Step 4: Set Permissions
Define what team managers can see:
- Screenshots
- Activity logs
- Reports
## Best Practices
- Keep teams small (5-15 members)
- Assign clear managers
- Review permissions quarterly
