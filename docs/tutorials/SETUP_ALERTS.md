# Tutorial: Setting Up Alerts

## Step 1: Navigate to Alerts

Settings > Alerts > Create Alert

## Step 2: Choose Trigger

- Idle time > 30 minutes
- Blocked site visited
- Working outside hours
- Low productivity

## Step 3: Set Notification

- Email
- Push notification
- Slack message
- Webhook

## Step 4: Assign Recipients

Select managers or teams to notify.

## Step 5: Activate

Toggle **Active** and click **Save**.
